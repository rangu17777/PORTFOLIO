# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Business owners in India and international founders/agencies looking to hire someone to build a website, automate manual work, or both. They arrive from Relentix, WhatsApp, or outreach and decide within a minute whether Sarang is credible and worth messaging.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Sarang Kumbhar, founder of Relentix (relentix.co.in), a web design studio. Positions him as a web designer/developer who also builds the automation behind a site (lead capture, follow-ups, CRM, AI agents). Success = a WhatsApp chat or email enquiry.

## Brand Personality
Confident, direct, builder. Plain language a shop owner understands, with enough technical proof to convince an international founder. Neon-dark visual identity stays as-is.

## Anti-references
Sci-fi terminal cosplay copy (META_PORTFOLIO, SYSTEM_ONLINE, "Transmission", "Sentient Systems", fake status chips), AI-generated cover art standing in for real work, "vibe coded" labels, "I don't sell websites".

## Design Principles
- Show real client work before claims.
- Websites and automation are one offer, not two.
- Every label must be understandable by a non-technical business owner.
- Keep the live demo: proof beats copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast for all content text; readable sizes on mobile; respect reduced motion where practical.
